  Rock Noise (ShaderNodeTexNoise)

    - Pattern will not be numerically identical.
    - Color output reuses the scalar noise.
  Rock Bump (ShaderNodeBump)
    Reason: Height-based bump approximated from screen-space derivatives (no height-to-normal expression is constructible from Python).

Equivalent (intent preserved, details differ):
  Texture Coordinate Object (ShaderNodeTexCoord)
    Reason: Coordinates converted to Blender conventions (UV V flipped; positions in meters, right-handed).
    - Blender's Generated coordinates are bounding-box normalized; Unreal local position is not.
  Principled BSDF (ShaderNodeBsdfPrincipled)
    Reason: Principled BSDF inputs map onto Unreal's Default Lit shading model; both are PBR but not identical.
    - Coat, sheen, subsurface and transmission are not mapped.
    - Alpha below 1 needs a translucent / masked blend mode.

NodeBridge reproduces procedural intent. Unless an operation is EXACT, expect a semantically equivalent system, not vertex- or pixel-identical output.
